Connecting to storage…
  • Unpowered board, harness unplugged, ohms range.
  • Connected = within about 1 Ω of your shorted-lead reading. Just press 0.
  • Not ~0? Swap red/black and fill the second box. Equal both ways = a resistor; different = through a chip.
  • MΩ, or a number that keeps climbing = open.
  • Accepted formats: 0, 0.4, 820, 7.4k, 1.02M, open.
